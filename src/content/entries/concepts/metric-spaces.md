A fundamental concept in functional analysis, playing a role similar to the $\mathbb{R}$ in calculus. 
A metric space is a set X with a *metric* on it. The metric associates with any pair of elements of X a *distnace*. 
Important additional properties which a metric space can have are completeness and seperability.
