The question of how to measure developer productivity (link to substack) is a complex one.  Experts caution against taking effort/output quantifiable and turning them into measures since the moment they become a goal in and of themselves, they are no longer valid. Measuring outcome/impact on the other hand is more ambiguous and otherwise, it is inherently a long-feedback loop. Following the suggestion of Andy, I propose a new methodology for myself, in which each day has its own reward model that works in the following way:
1. New Note - 3 Pts, given that it links to at least 3 other notes
2. New Link - 1 Point. 
I should create a notion table showcasing my daily points and plot these throughout time to promote good note taking behavior.
