# Daily log template

Paste the block below into your Notion daily-log template (Notion turns `##` into headings and `- [ ]` into to-do boxes). Then delete the old "Today will be good if" section.

Keep the template itself empty: the scorer counts any line written under a heading, so example text left in the template would be scored every day.

```
## Habits
- [ ] Meditation
- [ ] Contemplative walk
- [ ] Good writing session
- [ ] Cooking

## Numbers
Deep work (h):
Social media (min):
Drinks:

## Workout

## Articles Read

## Book Read

## Lecture

## What I Learned Summary

## TV Show/Movie

## Food

## Other Notes
```

## How to fill it in

**Habits**: tick what you did.
- **Good writing session:** a focused session on your own writing, about 45 minutes or more.
- **Contemplative walk:** a walk for thinking, not a commute.

**Numbers**: write the number right after the colon. Leave a line blank if it doesn't apply; blank scores zero.
- **Deep work (h):** hours of real focused work. Decimals are fine ("3.5").
- **Social media (min):** your phone's minutes, from Screen Time. Mac time is measured automatically.
- **Drinks:** alcoholic drinks.

**Sections**: one item per line.
- **Workout:** what you did. Anything written here counts as a workout.
- **Articles Read:** articles, papers and podcasts.
  - A paper is recognised by "arXiv", "paper" or an arXiv number in the line.
  - Sports podcasts by the show name (BS pod, Lowe Post…).
  - These lines also feed the public reading log.
- **Book Read:** the book and the page you reached ("Iron Flame, p. 140"). Not scored; it feeds the reading log.
- **Lecture:** any lesson or lecture you attended or studied, including chess.
- **What I Learned Summary:** a few lines on what you learned.
- **TV Show/Movie:** what you watched.
  - Your teams (Packers, Bucks, Brewers, NFL) and big live events are free.
  - So is anything with "Amit" in the line ("The Bear with Amit").
  - Highlights and recaps cost less than a show.
- **Food:** not scored; keep it if you like the record.
- **Other Notes:** anything else.
  - To give yourself points for something special, start a line with `Bonus` and the points: `Bonus 5: dinner with friends`.

## Captured automatically, with nothing to log
- **Anki reviews:** 20 or more in a day.
- **New notes in your vault:** AI Theory or Daily Learning.
- **Mac time on social media and in Sleeper:** measured by ActivityWatch.

## Where the points live
The Scorecard database on your Habit Tracker page in Notion holds what every line is worth. Change a number there and it applies from the next sync. To track something new, add a row. A `checklist` row matches a Habits box by its Match text; a `checklist-minutes` row matches a Numbers line.
